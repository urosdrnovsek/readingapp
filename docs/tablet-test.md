# Reading Room tablet test

Open the production build in Safari on iPad or Chrome on Android. The [README](../README.md) has the preview and installation commands. Wait for **Ready offline** before testing without a connection.

1. **Find a passage.** Choose English, Animals and Level 1. Search for **A Woven Nest** and open it. The title and passage should appear at a comfortable size, and returning to the library should preserve your filters.
2. **Try the markers.** Swipe over the text while the tray is in **Read** mode. Tap **Draw**, pick pink and draw across *bird carries*. Draw over part of the line again to deepen the colour. Tap **Read** to scroll. Open **Try this** and tap **Draw it** to sketch the nest below the story. Try the eraser and **Undo**.
3. **Listen.** Tap **Listen** on the marker tray. With a matching installed voice, playback should begin and underline the sentence. Tap **Stop**. For word playback and speed, open **Listen** from **Reading tools** and tap *carries*. A language without a matching voice should show an availability message.
4. **Resume offline.** Close **Listen**. Turn on **Guide** and use its arrows to reach the second sentence. Enlarge the text and rotate the tablet. Close the app, disconnect and reopen it. **Continue reading** should return to the same passage and preserve the highlight. Opening **Guide** should show sentence 2 of 4.
5. **Check VoiceOver or TalkBack.** Read the passage with the screen reader. Check that it follows the visible text in order when the colours and syllable breaks are on. Open **Choose words** from **Reading tools**, then use the labelled first- and last-word controls to save a mark.
6. **Find a favourite.** Check the estimated times on library titles. Save one title in English and one in another language. Open **Favourites** and check that both return after an offline restart. Begin **Guest reading** in **Settings** and check that it starts with an empty shelf. End the guest session to bring back your favourites.

For each issue, note the device, OS version, browser, passage language and what you tapped. Save the passage title if the issue occurs on one text.
