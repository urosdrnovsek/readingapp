# Reading Room tablet test

Open the production build in Safari on iPad or Chrome on Android. The [README](../README.md) has the preview and installation commands. Wait for **Ready offline** before testing without a connection.

1. **Find a passage.** Choose English, Animals and Level 1. Search for **A Woven Nest** and open it. The title and passage should appear at a comfortable size, and returning to the library should preserve your filters.
2. **Combine reading aids.** Tap **Highlight**, tap *bird*, then tap *carries* and save. In **Reading tools**, enable syllable colour, syllable breaks, and the **b** and **d** cues. The yellow highlight should remain, **b** and **d** should have their own colours, and *car·ries* should show a syllable break. Remove the mark in **Saved highlights** and restore it with **Undo**.
3. **Listen.** Turn on **Listen**, choose **Word**, and tap *carries*. Switch to **Sentence**, set the speed to **Slow**, and use **Play**, **Replay** and **Stop**. Repeat in another passage language with a matching installed voice. A language without a matching voice should show an availability message.
4. **Resume offline.** Close **Listen**. Turn on **Guide** and use its arrows to reach the second sentence. Enlarge the text and rotate the tablet. Close the app, disconnect and reopen it. **Continue reading** should return to the same passage and preserve the highlight. Opening **Guide** should show sentence 2 of 4.
5. **Check VoiceOver or TalkBack.** Read the passage with the screen reader. Check that it follows the visible text in order when the colours and syllable breaks are on. Use the labelled first- and last-word controls to save a highlight without tapping the words.
6. **Try a shared device.** Begin **Guest reading** from **Settings** and mark a different phrase. End the session. Your personal reading place and the original highlight should return.

For each issue, note the device, OS version, browser, passage language and what you tapped. Save the passage title if the issue occurs on one text.
